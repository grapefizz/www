I really wanna know what type of Walter White blue meth the folks over at EA were smoking during the making od this game.

Everyone and their dog has played Plants vs. Zombies, and with the highly anticipated release of Plants vs. Zombies Replanted,
lots of people had their hopes up, but then

<blockquote class="review negative">
<h4>MKDir <time datetime="2026-05-27">27/05/2026</time></h4>
I don't like PvZ Replanted. It got its soul sucked out. They just took the console version of PvZ ported it to pc and upscaled its textures with AI(Can't really tell them apart from afar but idk). The thing that immediately stuck out to me is that the bushes were always there and most of the original PvZ backgrounds were missing. I was really looking forward to playing it with my bro. Still gonna play it but ye wouldn't buy it at full price.
</blockquote>

<blockquote class="review negative">
<h4>Trin <time datetime="2026-05-31">31/05/2026</time></h4>
Overall a disappointing remaster. They did not make full utilization of existing features from other PvZ ports, such as achievements, gamemodes, etc. leaving the full game to feel incomplete.

There are a number of frustrating bugs and performance issues that do not exist in the original version of the game. If you can live without the quality of life improvements (upscaled art, fast-forward button on levels, and some added gamemodes) then I would recommend just playing the GOTY version for a fifth of the price.

If you like PvZ, you will like it. But unless you are extremely eager for new content, it’s not really worth it.
</blockquote>

Out of the total Steam 6,670 reviews, a whopping 64.99% of those reviews are negative. What went wrong?

Everything. End of blog, the whole thing is shit.

On a more serious note, what actually did go wrong? Well lets start with the build up to this game. Replanted has had a lot really 
high quality [teasers and trailers](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kZBc6FFI2gw) and this made the game look a lot more polished than it really was.
All of this lead to a lot of hype around the game, and after a long wait preorders went live and a week before release, the game was installed to every single person who bought it.
A few smart people found out a way to launch the game with a few file tweaks and the game was a mess.
